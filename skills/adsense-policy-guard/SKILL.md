# AdSense Policy Guard

Apply to every public page and UI change.

- Never fabricate data, reviews, authors, sources, statistics, or testimonials.
- Never promise guaranteed profits, guaranteed returns, or risk-free investing.
- Never encourage ad clicks or make ads resemble navigation, stock controls, downloads, or buttons.
- Keep ads visually distinct from content and avoid accidental-click layouts, especially on mobile.
- Do not create pages whose primary purpose is advertising.
- Do not publish scraped or lightly rewritten content as original.
- Keep ownership, contact, privacy, cookie, terms, and advertising disclosures accessible.
- Financial content must distinguish facts, data, analysis, and opinion.
- If live data is unavailable, say so; never invent a replacement.

Before publishing, verify user value, originality, factual accuracy, ad separation, mobile safety, and policy compliance.
