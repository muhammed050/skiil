# Entity SEO

- Maintain one consistent canonical entity identity across pages.
- Validate company name, ticker, exchange, sector, identifiers, and relationships.
- Avoid mixing data between similarly named companies or tickers.
- Connect stock, company, sector, market, and news pages with accurate internal links.
- Use Organization and other entity-related structured data only when supported.
- Keep names, logos, URLs, descriptions, and identifiers consistent.
- Never invent entity relationships, partnerships, ownership, awards, or credentials.
- Use stable URLs for entities and avoid duplicate entity pages.

Entity clarity must come from real data and consistent site architecture, not fabricated schema.
