# Financial Content Safety

- Separate market facts from analysis, opinion, and predictions.
- Never guarantee profits, returns, price targets, or future performance.
- Never call an investment risk-free.
- Use verified market data and accurate timestamps.
- Explain uncertainty when discussing forecasts or indicators.
- Clearly label educational content, analysis, sponsored content, and affiliate content.
- Do not fabricate analyst opinions, ratings, financial results, or news.
- Do not create manipulative urgency such as false breaking-news claims.
- Historical performance must not be presented as a promise of future results.
- If data is delayed or unavailable, state it explicitly.

Use neutral Turkish financial language and prioritize user understanding over sensationalism.
