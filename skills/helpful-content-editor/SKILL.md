# Helpful Content Editor

- Write primarily for users, not search engines.
- Use natural Turkish and clear headings, tables, examples, and explanations.
- Separate factual market data, historical information, analysis, commentary, opinion, and education.
- Never guarantee returns or future stock prices.
- Verify dates, numbers, company facts, and sources before publication.
- Avoid copied, spun, translated-without-value, or repetitive content.
- Programmatic pages must contain real entity-specific value; do not mass-produce thin pages.
- News must preserve source meaning and must not invent quotes or events.
- AI may draft or summarize but cannot be the source of truth for financial facts.
- Update stale information honestly and do not fake an updated date.
- Internal links must genuinely help users.

Quality gate: accuracy, user value, originality, clarity, transparency, then SEO.
