# News SEO

- Publish only real news with verifiable sources and publication times.
- Make headlines accurate, specific, and non-sensational.
- Preserve the meaning of source reporting.
- Never invent quotes, events, companies' statements, or breaking news.
- Clearly distinguish reporting from analysis and opinion.
- Use Article or NewsArticle structured data only when the page genuinely qualifies.
- Keep publication and modification dates truthful.
- Add author information only when it is real.
- Link to relevant companies, stocks, sectors, and background guides.
- Avoid republishing thin rewrites of other sites.
- Remove or archive obsolete pages thoughtfully rather than creating endless duplicates.

Freshness must reflect real updates, not artificial date changes.
