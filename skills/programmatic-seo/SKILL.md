# Programmatic SEO

Before generating a URL pattern, define its user intent, unique data, page purpose, canonical pattern, internal-link role, sitemap behavior, and minimum content quality.

Validate entity identity: company, ticker, exchange, sector, stable identifier, and source.

Stock pages should provide meaningful entity-specific information such as verified price status, change, volume, history, financial metrics, company context, sector, related news, related entities, and explanatory content when data exists.

Never mass-create pages containing only a ticker, price, and generic paragraph. Do not publish invalid or empty entities merely because a route can be generated.

Prevent duplicate URLs caused by parameters, casing, aliases, pagination, filters, and tracking parameters. Use canonical, redirects, noindex, or route constraints according to the actual intent.

Test a representative sample before scaling to thousands of URLs.
