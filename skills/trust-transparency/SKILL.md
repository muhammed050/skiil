# Trust Transparency

- Maintain accessible Hakkımızda, İletişim, Gizlilik Politikası, Çerez Politikası when applicable, and Kullanım Koşulları when applicable.
- Never invent licenses, partnerships, awards, experts, offices, authors, or credentials.
- Identify market-data source and status where practical: live, delayed, historical, unavailable, updating.
- Timestamps must represent real data retrieval or verification events.
- Distinguish news, analysis, opinion, education, and sponsored content.
- Clearly disclose affiliate or sponsored relationships.
- Privacy documentation must match actual analytics, advertising, authentication, hosting, and third-party services.
- Do not fabricate reviews, ratings, traffic, users, revenue, or popularity.
- Financial content must communicate uncertainty and investment risk without deceptive claims.

Prefer transparency over persuasion and real evidence over impressive-looking trust signals.
